Usability evaluation

Examine an existing product or prototype and turn usability concerns into specific recommendations with Kyle Hobson.

Discuss your project

When this helps

Your team needs to understand a difficult flow before deciding what to change or test.

What you receive

  • Agreed tasks and evaluation criteria
  • A structured record of observations and usability risks
  • Recommendations connected to the affected tasks
  • A review of priorities and questions for subsequent testing

How the work unfolds

We define the tasks and available product access. An expert review is useful for identifying risks; participant sessions answer questions about actual use.

I document the agreed evaluation, make observations and recommendations easy to compare, and review the next actions with your team.
