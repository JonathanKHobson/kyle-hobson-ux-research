Research synthesis

Turn qualitative material and existing findings into a useful product discussion with independent research synthesis.

Discuss your project

When this helps

Your team has collected material but still needs to understand what it means and how strongly it supports a decision.

What you receive

  • An inventory of the agreed evidence and its gaps
  • A coding or synthesis approach appropriate to the material
  • Themes linked to supporting evidence
  • Decision implications and a walkthrough

How the work unfolds

We inspect the source material, its provenance and its limits before choosing an approach.

I organize patterns and exceptions, separate interpretation from direct evidence, and explain how the synthesis changes the question.

Shape the scope

Bring to the conversation

  • A description of the material and its purpose
  • Existing research questions and previous findings
  • Permission and an agreed way to access the relevant sources

Agree before kickoff

We agree on access, privacy, source volume and the depth of analysis. Incomplete records will be identified; a synthesis does not make an unrepresentative sample representative.

Pricing and timing

I propose the scope, fee, schedule and review points after we discuss your project. Work starts once we agree on the deliverables and terms.
