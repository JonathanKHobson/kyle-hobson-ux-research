UX Research services

Discovery research

Explore your audience’s needs, context and barriers with a focused research study led by Kyle Hobson.

Discuss your project

When this helps

Your team has a product direction in mind but needs a stronger understanding of the people it is meant to serve.

What you receive

  • Research questions and a study plan
  • Agreed interview or survey materials and research execution
  • Findings with evidence, qualifications and unanswered questions
  • Recommendations and a team readout

How the work unfolds

We define the question, audience and decisions the study can support. I propose a method suited to the available access and scope.

I examine the results, distinguish reported behavior from observed behavior, and work through the implications with your team.

Shape the scope

Bring to the conversation

  • The decision your team needs to make
  • What you already know about the audience
  • Available audience access and research material

Agree before kickoff

We agree on methods, participant criteria, recruitment responsibilities, consent, incentives, information handling and analysis depth. Large-scale statistical validation is a distinct scope.

Pricing and timing

I propose the scope, fee, schedule and review points after we discuss your project. Work starts once we agree on the deliverables and terms.
